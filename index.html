Where do my downloads go?

Device storage guide

  1. Open the "Files" App: Look for the blue folder icon on your home screen (or search for "Files").
  2. Browse Downloads: Tap the Browse tab → tap Downloads (or check Recents).
  3. Save to Specific Folder: When the PDF opens in Safari, tap the Share icon (↑) and select "Save to Files" to pick any iCloud or device folder, or tap "Books" to keep it in Apple Books.
  1. Default Folder: Files save automatically to your phone's Downloads folder.
  2. Find Your File: Open Files by Google or your phone's File Manager → tap Downloads.
  3. Notification: Swipe down from the top of your screen to tap the finished download notification directly.
  1. Default Folder: Saved to your computer's Downloads directory.
  2. Choose Folder (Chrome / Edge): Use the "Choose Folder" button in the download popup to pick any specific folder or USB drive.
  3. Shortcut: Press Ctrl + J (Windows) or Cmd + Option + L (Mac) to view browser downloads.
